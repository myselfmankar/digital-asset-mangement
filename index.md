## 🌟 **What is PhotoStack AI?**

PhotoStack AI is an intelligent photo management system that uses artificial intelligence to automatically organize your entire photo collection. Think of it as having a personal assistant who understands every photo you've ever taken.

### 🎯 **The Problem**

> You have **10,000+ photos** scattered across devices and folders. Finding that perfect sunset from your California vacation means scrolling for 20 minutes. Manual organization? Too time-consuming.

### ✨ **The Solution**

> Just ask: *"Show me sunset photos from California in 2023"* and get instant, accurate results. No folders to create. No tags to manually assign. Pure AI-powered intelligence.

## 🧠 **How It Works**

### **Step 1: Upload Your Photos**
Drag and drop any photo—from your phone, camera, or computer. The system handles all formats automatically.

### **Step 2: AI Analysis (Automatic)**
Every photo is analyzed in the background:
- **Visual Understanding:** What's in the photo?
- **Metadata Extraction:** Camera settings, GPS location, date taken
- **Tag Generation:** Automatic descriptive tags
- **Location Lookup:** GPS coordinates → "Malibu, California"

### **Step 3: Search & Discover**
Now your entire collection is searchable:
- Type natural questions: "summer vacation photos"
- Filter by date, location, camera, or tags
- Browse chronological albums
- Explore photos on an interactive map

### **The Magic: Two-Tier AI Search**
1. **Smart Mode:** AI understands your question and finds exact matches
2. **Fuzzy Mode:** If nothing matches perfectly, it searches broadly
3. **Result:** You always find what you're looking for

## 🚀 **Quick Start**

### **Requirements**
- Docker installed on your computer
- Google Gemini API key ([Get one free here](https://ai.google.dev/))

### **Setup (3 minutes)**

**1. Download the project**
```bash
git clone https://github.com/yourusername/photostack-ai.git
cd photostack-ai
```

**2. Add your AI key**
```bash
cp env.txt .env
# Edit .env and add: GEMINI_API_KEY=your_key_here
```

**3. Start the app**
```bash
docker-compose up --build
```

**4. Open in browser**
Visit: [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)

That's it! Start uploading photos and watch the AI work its magic. ✨
